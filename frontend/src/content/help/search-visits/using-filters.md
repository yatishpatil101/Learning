The [Listings page](/listings) has a lot of filters. Applying them in the wrong order is why searches return either 400 results or zero.

## Apply filters in this order

### 1. Locality or commute, not city

Pune is too large to search as one unit. Either pick 2–3 localities, or use **commute filter** — enter your workplace and a maximum travel time, and we filter to what is genuinely reachable. See [Map view and commute time](/help/a/map-view-commute).

### 2. Budget as a range, not a ceiling

Set both a minimum and a maximum. A ₹0–₹30,000 search buries the ₹28,000 flats you want under the ₹9,000 ones you do not.

> [!TIP]
> Set the maximum about 10% above your real ceiling. Rent is negotiable more often than people assume, and excluding a property at ₹33,000 that would go for ₹30,000 costs you options.

### 3. Configuration and carpet area

BHK alone is unreliable — a 2 BHK in Baner and a 2 BHK in Kothrud can differ by 300 sq ft. If space matters, filter on **carpet area** instead.

### 4. The deal-breakers only

Furnishing, parking, pet-friendly, vegetarian-only, gated society, lift, power backup. Add these one at a time and watch the result count. If it collapses, you have found the constraint that is actually costing you.

## Filters worth knowing about

- **Available from** — hides properties that free up after your move-in date
- **Posted by owner** — already the default; every PuneNest listing is owner-posted
- **Verified only** — restricts to listings that passed [verification](/help/a/how-we-verify)
- **Freshness** — surfaces listings confirmed available in the last 7 days

## Sorting

Default sort is relevance, which blends freshness, verification and completeness. Switch to **Newest** while actively hunting — in a hot locality, being early matters more than being precise.
