## Booking

From any property page, tap **Schedule visit**, pick a date and a time slot, and confirm. The owner is notified immediately and confirms or proposes an alternative.

You will get a reminder the evening before and an hour ahead. Manage all bookings under **Dashboard → Visits**.

You can hold up to **three** unconfirmed visits at a time. Cancel the ones you no longer want — no-shows affect how owners treat later requests from your account.

## Before you travel

- Confirm the exact unit number, not just the building
- Ask whether the current tenant is still in place
- Check the [locality page](/locality) for the commute at that hour

## What to check on site

### The unit

- Water pressure in every tap, and hot water
- Damp patches on ceilings and shared walls — check corners
- Sunlight and cross-ventilation at the time of day you are usually home
- Mobile signal in each room
- Number and position of power points
- Actual usable space in wardrobes and kitchen storage

### The building

- Lift availability and backup for it
- Water supply hours and whether there is a borewell
- Power backup — full or lights-only
- Parking: covered or open, and whether it is allotted
- Security arrangement and visitor process

### The terms

- Rent, deposit and notice period
- Maintenance — who pays, and how much
- What happens to the deposit at exit, and over what timeline
- Any restriction on guests, pets or cooking

> [!TIP]
> Take photos of anything already damaged, and share them with the owner in writing on the day you move in. Undocumented pre-existing damage is the most common cause of deposit disputes.

## If the owner does not show up

Report the no-show from the visit card in your dashboard. Repeated no-shows lead to the listing being suspended, and you get priority support to find alternatives in the same locality.
