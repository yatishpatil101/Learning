Most people pick a locality first and a building second. Draazy has a page for each, so you can do that research without leaving the site.

## Locality pages

[Locality insights](/locality) covers Pune's main areas with prices, how they have moved, rental yield, livability and the inventory currently live on Draazy.

Ten localities are covered in depth; the rest carry whatever we can say honestly. Where we have no data, the page says so rather than filling the gap.

From any locality page you can jump straight into [search](/listings) restricted to that area, or set an alert so new listings there reach you. See [Saved searches and alerts](/help/a/saved-searches-alerts).

## Society pages

[Explore societies](/societies) lists Pune housing societies. Search, filter by locality, and follow the ones you are interested in.

Each society has a hub with five tabs:

| Tab | What is on it |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Specs, amenities, age, and a summary rating |
| Homes | Flats currently on sale or on rent in that building, on Draazy |
| Reviews | Ratings, with a resident badge where we could confirm the reviewer lives there |
| Community | Questions and answers, tips, notices and events |
| Location | Map, connectivity and what is nearby |

> [!TIP]
> Follow a society even when nothing is listed in it. Following is how we know demand exists for a building, and it is what puts a new listing there in front of you first.

## Contributing

Anyone signed in can follow a society, leave a review, ask a question or answer one. There is no identity check for any of that — see [How we verify](/help/a/how-we-verify).

## If you actually live there

Confirming you are a resident of that building — your flat, confirmed by OTP or by the committee — is separate from identity verification, and it unlocks more:

- Your reviews carry a **resident** badge
- You can post notices and events to the community tab
- You can propose a correction to the building's details, its location pin or its WhatsApp group link

Proposals are read by our team before they go live, because a building's page is shared by everyone who lives in it.

## If your society is missing

Add it from [Explore societies](/societies). A society added this way is marked community-added until it is confirmed, and it works normally in the meantime — you can follow it, review it and list a flat in it.
