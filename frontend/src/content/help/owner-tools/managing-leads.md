Every enquiry lands in **Dashboard → Enquiries**, grouped by property.

## The one number that matters

**Response time.** Tenants contact several owners at once and commit to the first credible one. Replying within two hours roughly doubles your conversion versus replying the next day.

Turn on push and SMS alerts for new enquiries under [notification settings](/help/a/notifications-language).

## Triaging

Each enquiry shows the tenant's [profile](/help/a/manage-profile) — occupation, household, move-in date, pets, and whether their number is verified. Use it:

- **Move-in date** — if it is three months out and you need someone next week, say so immediately rather than going quiet
- **Household composition** — matches or does not match what the flat suits
- **Verified number** — unverified enquiries are worth less of your time

Mark each as **Interested**, **Visit scheduled**, **Not a fit** or **Closed** so the list stays usable.

## Templates

Save reply templates under **Enquiries → Templates** for the three messages you will send constantly:

- Available, here are the terms, when can you visit
- Not available until *date*, would that work
- Already rented, thank you

> [!TIP]
> When you mark a property rented, all open enquiries are auto-notified and the listing is delisted. Doing this promptly is the main thing that keeps your account in good standing — and stale listings get demoted platform-wide.

## Site visits

Visit requests appear under **Dashboard → Visits**. Confirm or propose an alternative. Batch visits into one or two windows per week rather than one at a time.

## Closing

Once you have chosen a tenant:

1. Agree terms in writing in the message thread — rent, deposit, notice, maintenance
2. Get the [rent agreement](/services/rent-agreement) drafted and registered
3. Do a joint handover with photos and meter readings
4. Mark the property rented
