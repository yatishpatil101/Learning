Every enquiry carries the tenant's profile. It is the closest thing to screening you get before a call.

## What the profile contains

| Field | Verified by us? |
| --- | --- |
| Name | No — self-declared |
| Mobile number | **Yes** — OTP |
| Government ID | Yes, if the tenant completed KYC |
| Occupation & employer | No |
| Household composition | No |
| Move-in date | No |
| Pets | No |
| Previous tenancy | No |

> [!NOTE]
> A verified number and completed KYC tell you the person is real and traceable. Everything else is self-declared — verify it yourself before signing.

## Sensible verification before signing

- **Employment** — offer letter or the last three months' salary slips
- **Identity** — Aadhaar or passport, matched to the person in front of you
- **Previous landlord** — a two-minute call, which owners underuse and which is the most informative check available
- **Bank statement** — reasonable for a high-value property, intrusive for a modest one

Ask for these once you have shortlisted, not in the first message.

## What you may not filter on

> [!WARNING]
> Listing preferences that exclude tenants by **religion, caste, region, marital status or disability** are removed, and repeated attempts lead to account suspension.

You *can* state genuine, property-related conditions: vegetarian-only kitchens where the society mandates it, no pets, family-only occupancy, working professionals only, no smoking.

The distinction is whether the condition relates to how the property is used or to who the person is.

## Police verification

Tenant police verification is mandatory in Maharashtra. Submit Form for Tenant Verification at the local police station or online through the Maharashtra Police portal, within 15 days of the tenancy starting. It is the owner's obligation, not the tenant's.
