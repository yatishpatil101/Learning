[Pay rent](/pay-rent) lets you send rent directly to your landlord's bank account and keeps the paper trail for you.

## Setting it up

1. Open [Pay rent](/pay-rent).
2. Add your landlord's name, bank account or UPI ID, and the rent amount.
3. Add the property address — this appears on the receipt and matters for HRA.
4. Choose one-time or monthly on a fixed date.
5. Pay.

The money settles to the landlord's account. Neither party needs a PuneNest plan.

## Why do it here rather than a plain bank transfer

- **HRA receipts** are generated automatically, with the address and landlord name your employer needs
- **A dated payment history** you can produce in a deposit dispute
- **Reminders** before the due date
- **Card payments** work even where the landlord only accepts bank transfers

## Fees

- **UPI and net banking** — no convenience fee
- **Credit and debit cards** — a gateway fee applies, shown before you confirm

> [!TIP]
> Paying rent by credit card to earn points rarely works out once the gateway fee is counted. Check the fee against the reward value before you do it habitually.

## HRA and landlord PAN

To claim HRA above ₹1,00,000 of rent per financial year, Indian tax rules require your landlord's PAN. Add it under **Rent → Landlord details** and it appears on every receipt.

If the landlord will not share their PAN, you can still claim by filing Form 60 declaration details with your employer — but get the refusal in writing.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails but you were charged, the amount is auto-reversed by the gateway, usually within 3–5 working days. If it does not appear, raise a ticket at [Support](/support) with the transaction reference.
