Your profile lives under [Dashboard](/dashboard). Some of it is private; some of it is what owners judge you on.

## What owners can see

When you enquire on a listing, the owner sees:

- Your name and mobile number
- Whether your number is OTP-verified
- Your **tenant profile**, if you have filled one in

They do **not** see your email, your saved properties, your search history, or which other owners you have contacted.

## Your tenant profile

A completed [tenant profile](/tenant-profile) is the single highest-leverage thing a renter can do on Draazy. Owners get many enquiries and reply to the ones they can evaluate.

Worth filling in:

- Occupation and workplace locality
- Family or flatmate composition
- Move-in date and intended stay
- Whether you have pets
- A one-line introduction

> [!TIP]
> Profiles with a move-in date and an occupation get noticeably more owner responses than bare enquiries. It costs two minutes.

## Changing your mobile number

Your number is your login, so changing it needs OTP verification on both the old and new numbers. Go to **Dashboard → Account → Change number**. If you no longer have access to the old number, raise a ticket at [Support](/support) with ID proof.

## Deleting your account

**Dashboard → Account → Delete account.** This removes your profile, saved properties, searches and messages. Listings you posted are delisted. Invoices and tickets are retained for the period required by law — see [Your data and privacy](/help/a/your-data-privacy).
