Your profile lives under **Dashboard > Profile**. Some of it is private; some of it is what owners judge you on.

## What owners can see

When you enquire on a listing, the owner sees:

- Your name and a masked version of your mobile number
- Whether your number is OTP-verified
- Your **tenant profile**, if you have filled one in

Your mobile is used for the contact request. The owner sees the full number only after approving the request. They do **not** see your email, your saved properties, your search history, or which other owners you have contacted.

## Your tenant profile

A completed tenant profile is the single highest-leverage thing a renter can do on Draazy. Owners get many enquiries and reply to the ones they can evaluate.

Worth filling in:

- Occupation
- Monthly income, if you choose to add it
- Who will live in the home
- Move-in date
- Prior landlord reference
- A short introduction

> [!TIP]
> Profiles with a move-in date and an occupation get noticeably more owner responses than bare enquiries. It costs two minutes.

## Changing your mobile number

Your number is your login, so there is no self-serve switch for it. Raise a support ticket; support will tell you what proof is needed.

## Deleting your account

**Dashboard > Profile > Privacy & Account > Request account erasure** files an erasure request for review. If it is approved, your profile is removed and your account is erased where the law allows. Records such as invoices, legal documents, tickets and audit logs may be retained where required - see [Your data and privacy](/help/a/your-data-privacy).
