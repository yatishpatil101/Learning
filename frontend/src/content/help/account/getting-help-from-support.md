Most things on Draazy are self-serve. When they are not, [Support](/support) is the way through — it gives you a ticket number and a thread you can come back to, which email and phone calls do not.

## Raising a ticket

1. Go to [Support](/support) and pick a **category**: Payments & refunds, Rent payment / HRA, Property listing, Verification / KYC, Account & login, Visit / booking, Home services, Technical / bug, or Something else.
2. Write a subject and describe what happened.
3. Set a **priority** if the default of Normal is wrong.
4. Attach up to **four screenshots**. They are compressed before upload, so a phone photo is fine.
5. Submit. You get a ticket id in the form `SUP-10042` — quote it in anything you send us.

Your name, mobile and email are filled in from your account. The mobile has to be a valid 10-digit Indian number, because it is how we find you.

## What the statuses mean

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| New | Raised, not yet picked up |
| In progress | Someone is on it |
| Awaiting your reply | We have asked you something and are waiting |
| Resolved | We believe it is fixed |
| Closed | Finished |

> [!NOTE]
> Resolved and Closed are not final. Replying to a closed ticket re-opens it — you never have to raise a duplicate to continue a conversation.

## Things only support can do

Some outcomes are deliberately not self-served, because letting either side set them would make them worthless:

- Marking a visit as a **no-show** — see [Scheduling a visit](/help/a/schedule-a-visit)
- Reversing a verification decision
- Anything touching a refund

For these, raise a ticket rather than looking for a button.

## Before you raise one

Check the [FAQs](/help/faq) first, and the rest of this help centre. A ticket that turns out to be answered here costs you a day of waiting.

If the problem is a listing rather than your account — a wrong price, a broker posing as an owner, a property that does not exist — use **Report this listing** on the listing itself instead. See [Reporting a listing](/help/a/report-a-listing).
