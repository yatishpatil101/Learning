## How contact works

Open a listing and tap **Contact owner**. You need to be signed in with a verified mobile number — that is the only requirement, and it is the same floor everywhere on Draazy.

Tapping does not hand you a phone number. It sends the owner a **contact request**, and their number stays partly masked (`+91 98xxx xxxx02`) until they say yes. A request sits in one of three states:

| State | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Request sent. The owner has not answered yet. |
| Approved | The full number is revealed, and you can call or message. |
| Declined | The owner passed. This is final for that listing. |

Tapping again on the same listing does not send a second request — it shows you where the first one stands.

> [!NOTE]
> Some owners prefer to keep their number private even after approving. There you will see **Approved — owner prefers in-app chat**, and the conversation stays in the app.

## Do I need to be identity-verified?

No. The **Verified** badge is a trust signal, not a gate — you can search, enquire, schedule visits and post without one.

The single exception is an owner who has switched on **verified enquirers only**. On those listings you are sent to [identity verification](/verify-identity) first: one government ID and one live selfie, reviewed by a person. Submitting is not the same as passing, so allow for the review before you plan around it.

## How many owners can I contact?

Owner contacts are capped per account, not per day:

| Plan | Owner contacts |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 15 in total |
| Any paid plan | Unlimited |
| Referral reward | +15 on top of the free allowance, per qualified referral |

The count is a **lifetime total**, not a monthly or daily refill. One listing costs one contact however many times you open it, and whether the owner approves or declines does not change the number — you are charged for asking, once, per property.

When the allowance runs out the app says so and offers a plan or a referral link. See what is left any time from [Plans](/plans).

## Writing an enquiry that gets answered

Owners get many enquiries and approve the ones that look serious. Your message is attached to the request, so it is the thing they decide on. A good first message answers the three things they are silently asking:

1. **Who are you?** Occupation, family or flatmate composition.
2. **When do you need it?** A specific date, not "soon".
3. **Are you actually in range?** Confirm you have seen the rent and deposit.

A workable template:

> Hi, I'm interested in your 2 BHK in Baner. I work at a product company in Hinjawadi, moving with my wife, and we're looking to shift by 15 August. The rent and deposit work for us. Could we visit this weekend — Saturday morning or Sunday afternoon?

> [!TIP]
> Fill in your [tenant profile](/tenant-profile) before you enquire. Owners see it attached to your request, and it answers most of their questions before they have to ask.

## If the request stays pending

- Give it 24 hours — most owners are not full-time landlords.
- Check the listing's freshness badge; an unconfirmed listing may already be rented.
- Use [Report this listing](/help/a/report-a-listing) if it is clearly gone, so other tenants do not waste time on it.

A declined request cannot be re-sent. That is deliberate: it stops a refusal being worn down by repetition.

## Staying safe

Keep negotiations in the app's message thread where possible. If a conversation moves to WhatsApp and then to money before a visit, re-read [Spotting a rental scam](/help/a/spot-a-scam).
