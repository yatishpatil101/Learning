## Why 11 months?

Under the Registration Act, a lease of **12 months or more must be registered**. An 11-month agreement avoids that requirement, which is why it became the default across India. It is renewable, and renewal is routine.

## Registration in Maharashtra

Maharashtra is stricter than most states. Section 55 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act requires **every tenancy agreement to be registered**, regardless of duration.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> An unregistered rent agreement is not admissible as primary evidence in a Maharashtra court. If a dispute over deposit or possession reaches a court, the unregistered document severely weakens your position. Register it.

Costs, as a rough guide:

- **Stamp duty** — 0.25% of the total rent plus deposit for the term
- **Registration fee** — ₹1,000 for urban areas
- **Service/facilitation** — varies

## What the agreement must contain

- Full names and addresses of both parties
- Complete property address including unit number
- Term: start and end date
- Rent, due date and payment mode
- Security deposit and refund timeline
- Notice period for both parties
- Lock-in period, if any
- Maintenance responsibility, itemised
- Escalation on renewal
- Permitted use, and any restriction on subletting
- Signatures of both parties and two witnesses

## Common mistakes

- Signing with blank fields to be "filled in later"
- No refund timeline for the deposit
- Maintenance described as "as applicable" rather than a number
- Only the tenant bound by the notice period
- Wrong or incomplete unit number
