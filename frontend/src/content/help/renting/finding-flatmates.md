[Flatmates](/flatmates) covers three situations: you have a room to fill, you want a room in someone's flat, or you want to team up with strangers to rent a whole flat together. Each room, group and seeker post has its own detail page.

You only need to be signed in to post or to show interest. There is no identity gate here either — verification is a badge, not a door.

## Posting a room

1. Go to [Flatmates](/flatmates) and choose **A room in my place**.
2. Say whether you are the **owner** of the flat or a **sitting tenant** looking for a replacement or an extra person.
3. Add the property, the room kind, and whether the room is **Single** or **Shared**. A shared room's rent is split equally between the people sharing it.
4. State your preferences honestly — food habits, working hours, guests, smoking.
5. Add a headline, photos and a note. The headline is yours, capped at 120 characters; the app can suggest one.
6. Publish. Interested people message you in-app; your number is not shown until you choose to share it.

Rooms posted by the flat's owner usually go live straight away, because the property has already passed listing review. Rooms posted by tenants, and flagged addresses, wait for our team to read the post before the public board shows them. You can still see and edit your post while it waits.

## What the host badge means

Every post carries a host standing, derived by us and never claimed by the poster:

| Host | How it is earned |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | You attached one of your own listings that our team has already verified |
| Tenant | You are a sitting tenant and attached your **registered** rent agreement, and a person checked it |
| No badge | Everything else — including an owner without a verified listing, or a tenant who declared an agreement but did not upload it |

A tenant's claim to live in the flat can be rejected with a reason, and editing the flat or the claim starts the check again. Publishing a post and granting a badge are separate reviews: a live post does not earn a badge, and a badge does not publish a post.

### If you are a sitting tenant

As well as the agreement, you confirm the flat owner knows: enter the owner's mobile, and they receive an OTP to approve. That turns "trust me, the owner is fine with it" into something we can actually point at.

> [!NOTE]
> None of this is required to post. It changes how seriously people take your post, not whether you can make one.

## Looking for a room

Filter by locality, budget share, room type and the household profile. Every listing shows who already lives there and what they are looking for. Tap **Send interest**; after the host accepts, the button becomes **Message owner** and opens Messages.

> [!TIP]
> Ask to visit at the time you would normally be home. A flat that is quiet at 3 pm on a Wednesday tells you very little about weeknights.

## Team up and groups

If you do not have a flat yet, choose **Still looking for a flat**. A group can list up to three preferred localities, BHK, rent and deposit ranges, furnishing, move-in timing and whether bachelors are okay. It appears on **Team up** so people can form the household first.

If you already have a flat, choose **I have a flat** and post the seats you want to fill. A group advertises how many seats it has and how many are still open, so nobody joins a flat that is already full. If the policy is open, joining is instant; otherwise the host accepts or declines each request.

Members can leave, cancel a pending request, and the host can remove members. Group chat lives in [Messages](/messages). When a group is ready to apply to a whole-flat rental, the owner sees one application with all profiles attached.

A person can be in only a limited number of active groups at once, including pending requests. The default cap is two.

## What to agree before moving in

Put this in writing between yourselves, even informally:

- Rent share and who holds the master agreement
- Deposit share and how it returns when someone leaves
- Notice period between flatmates — one month is standard
- Bills: how they split, and who pays them
- Guests and overnight visitors
- Cleaning and any shared help

## Safety

- Meet in the flat, in daylight, before committing.
- Verify that whoever is collecting deposit money is actually on the rent agreement.
- Never pay a deposit to an outgoing flatmate you have not met — settle it with the owner or the lead tenant.

> [!WARNING]
> Discriminating against a flatmate applicant on the basis of religion, caste, region or marital status is not permitted in listing terms. Preferences about food habits and working hours are fine; identity-based exclusions are removed.

Brokers are not welcome on this board, and the anti-broker checks are why a post can be held for a human to read — for example when two different people claim the same address. Slipping a phone number, email or messaging handle into free text is refused; use the interest and Messages flow instead.
