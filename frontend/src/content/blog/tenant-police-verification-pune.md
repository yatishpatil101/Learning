If you rent out a flat in Pune, you must give your tenant's details to the police. Many owners think this is optional or that registering the rent agreement covers it. It does not, and Pune Police regularly take action against owners who skip it.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This is general information, not legal advice. Police procedures change, so confirm the current process on the official websites linked below.

## Is tenant verification mandatory in Pune?

Yes. The Pune City Police issue a standing order under **Section 163 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)**, which replaced Section 144 of the old CrPC. It requires owners, and estate agents who arrange rentals, to submit tenant details to the police. The order is reissued every couple of months, so it is always in force.

The same rule applies to paying guest (PG) accommodation and to flats in Pimpri Chinchwad, which has its own police commissionerate.

## What happens if you skip it?

Owners who do not submit tenant details can face action under **Section 223 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)** for disobeying a public servant's order. This can mean a fine, imprisonment or both.

This is not a rule on paper only. In July and August 2026, Pune Police acted against well over a hundred landlords and PG operators who had not submitted tenant details, after special checking drives.

## How to submit tenant details online

### Pune City Police

Go to the [Pune City Police website](https://punepolice.gov.in) and open the citizen services section. Look for the tenant or paying guest information form, fill it in and upload the documents. Keep the acknowledgement it gives you.

### Pimpri Chinchwad Police

For flats under the Pimpri Chinchwad Police, which covers areas such as Wakad, Hinjawadi, Pimple Saudagar and Ravet, use the [Pimpri Chinchwad Police tenant information page](https://pcpc.gov.in/tenant-info). You can submit it online, at the local police station or by registered post. An OTP is sent to the owner's mobile number to confirm the submission.

> [!NOTE]
> You do not need a police "NOC" to rent out your flat. You only need to inform the police about your tenant.

## Documents to keep ready

Details vary slightly between the two forms, but keep these handy:

- **Owner:** name, mobile number, address and ID proof.
- **Tenant:** a recent photo, Aadhaar or another photo ID, permanent address, and workplace or college details.
- **Rented flat:** the full address.
- **Rent agreement:** a copy of the registered leave and licence agreement.

If several adults will live in the flat, give details for each of them.

## Does online agreement registration cover this?

Partly. When a leave and licence agreement is registered online through the government's registration system, the details are shared with the police. Even so, Pune Police advise owners to submit tenant details directly as well. It takes a few minutes and removes any doubt, so do both.

## When to do it

Submit the details before the tenant moves in, or on the day they do. Do it again whenever a new tenant or flatmate moves in. Keep the acknowledgement with your copy of the agreement.
