Thousands of students and young professionals move to Pune every year, and most face the same first choice: a **PG** (paying guest accommodation) or a **room in a shared flat**. Neither is better for everyone. It depends on your budget, how long you plan to stay and how much you want to manage yourself.

## At a glance

- **PG:** move in quickly with a small deposit. Meals, cleaning and Wi-Fi are often included. Expect less privacy and more house rules.
- **Shared flat:** more space, privacy and freedom. Expect a bigger deposit, shared bills and more to organise.

## A PG: easy to start

A PG is the quickest way to land in a new city. You usually pay one monthly amount that covers your bed, and often food, housekeeping, electricity and Wi-Fi.

**Good for:** freshers, students and anyone on a short assignment, or who wants to settle in before choosing an area.

**Watch out for:**

- **Rules** on guests, entry times and cooking.
- **Sharing:** many PGs put two or three people in a room.
- **Hidden charges** for electricity, laundry or food. Ask for every charge in writing.
- **Notice period and deposit refund terms.** Get these in writing too.

## A shared flat: more room to live

Sharing a 2BHK or 3BHK with friends or colleagues usually gives you your own room, a kitchen and no house rules beyond the society's. It often works out cheaper per person than a good PG, especially in a group of three or four.

**Good for:** people staying for a year or more, and anyone who wants to cook, work from home or have guests.

**Watch out for:**

- **A larger security deposit,** split between flatmates. Read our [security deposit guide](/blog/security-deposit-pune).
- **Shared bills** for electricity, gas, Wi-Fi and a cook or maid. Agree in advance how you will split them.
- **What happens when someone leaves.** Decide who finds the replacement and how their share of the deposit is returned.

## The paperwork

**For a shared flat,** the owner and tenants sign a leave-and-licence agreement, which must be registered in Maharashtra. Our [rent agreement guide](/blog/rent-agreement-pune) explains how. It is sensible for every flatmate to be named in it. If only one person is named, the others have little to show if there is a dispute.

**For a PG,** ask for a written agreement or receipt that shows the rent, deposit, notice period and what is included.

## Police verification applies to both

Pune City Police and Pimpri Chinchwad Police require owners and PG operators to share tenant and resident details with the police. In 2026 both forces ran drives against landlords and PG operators who had not done so. A registered leave-and-licence agreement is shared with the police system automatically.

As a tenant, keep a copy of your ID and agreement handy, and make sure your details have been submitted. Our guide to [tenant police verification in Pune](/blog/tenant-police-verification-pune) explains the process.

## Is GST charged on PG rent?

Usually not for long stays. Since July 2024, accommodation costing **₹20,000 or less per person per month** and taken for at least **90 days** in a row has been exempt from GST. Shorter stays or higher rents may attract GST, so ask your PG how it bills.

## Before you pay anything

1. **Visit in person** and see the exact room you will get.
2. **Check what is included** and what costs extra.
3. **Read the deposit and notice terms** and keep them in writing.
4. **Never pay a token amount** for a place you have not seen. Learn the [warning signs of rental scams](/blog/rental-scams-pune).
