## At a glance

- **Civic body:** Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC).
- **Known for:** one of Pune's largest and most settled residential suburbs, with many schools, colleges, hospitals and shops.
- **Homes:** a wide mix, from older housing societies to newer apartment buildings.
- **Best for:** families who want an established neighbourhood close to the city centre.

## Who it suits

Kothrud has long been a favourite with Pune families. It has a strong choice of schools and colleges, good hospitals and everyday shopping within easy reach, and it is close to Deccan, Karve Road and the older city. Students and professionals working in the west and centre of Pune also find it convenient.

## Getting around

- **Metro:** Kothrud has the advantage of an operational metro. Vanaz, Anand Nagar and Ideal Colony on Line 2 connect it to Deccan, the railway station and Ramwadi. An extension from Vanaz to Chandani Chowk was approved in June 2025.
- **Roads:** Paud Road and Karve Road are the main routes into the city. Chandani Chowk connects to the Mumbai–Bengaluru highway.
- **To Hinjawadi:** possible via Chandani Chowk and the highway, but test it at peak hours.

## Daily life

Kothrud is busy and well served, with markets, restaurants and green spaces such as the hills around Vetal Tekdi nearby. Traffic on the main roads is heavy at peak hours.

## Before you rent or buy

1. **Check how far you are from a metro station** if you will rely on it.
2. **For older buildings, ask about repairs,** lifts and the society's maintenance fund.
3. **Check parking,** which can be limited in older societies.
4. **For a resale flat, get the Index II and title papers checked.** See [Index II explained](/blog/index-2-pune-property).

## Nearby alternatives

[Aundh](/locality/aundh) and [Baner](/locality/baner) are to the north. Karve Nagar, Erandwane and Bavdhan are close by, with Bavdhan offering newer societies.
