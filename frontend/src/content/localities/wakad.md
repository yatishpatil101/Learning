## At a glance

- **Civic body:** Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC).
- **Police:** Pimpri Chinchwad Police.
- **Known for:** large gated societies right next to the Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park in Hinjawadi.
- **Best for:** families and working couples who want a short commute and a full neighbourhood.

## Who it suits

Wakad grew alongside the Hinjawadi IT park, and many people who work there choose it for the balance it offers: a reasonable commute with schools, hospitals, shops and a wide choice of societies. It suits families in particular, and couples who want more space than they would get closer to the city.

## Getting around

- **Roads:** Wakad has quick access to the Mumbai–Bengaluru highway bypass and the Mumbai–Pune Expressway, which makes weekend trips to Mumbai or Lonavala easy.
- **Metro:** Wakad Chowk is a station on Pune Metro Line 3 (Hinjawadi to Shivajinagar). The line was not yet open as of early October 2026.
- **To Hinjawadi:** short, but the roads into the IT park are busy at peak hours, especially around the highway junctions.

## Daily life

Wakad has a good range of schools, colleges, supermarkets and restaurants, and the Phoenix Mall of the Millennium for shopping and films. Because it is so popular, traffic inside the area can be heavy in the evenings.

## Before you rent or buy

1. **Do your commute at office time,** both ways.
2. **Ask about water supply.** Some societies around Hinjawadi rely partly on tankers.
3. **Compare a few societies.** Rents and amenities vary sharply.
4. **Register the agreement and inform the police.** Wakad falls under the Pimpri Chinchwad Police. See our guide to [tenant police verification](/blog/tenant-police-verification-pune).

## Nearby alternatives

[Hinjawadi](/locality/hinjawadi) is closer to the offices, [Pimple Saudagar](/locality/pimple-saudagar) is a settled residential option nearby, and [Balewadi](/locality/balewadi) and [Baner](/locality/baner) bring you closer to the city. Tathawade and Punawale are newer areas just beyond Wakad.
