## At a glance

- **Civic body:** Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC).
- **Known for:** EON IT Park and the World Trade Center, two of east Pune's largest office campuses.
- **Homes:** mostly newer gated societies and high-rises.
- **Best for:** IT and finance professionals working in Kharadi, and families who want a newer flat.

## Who it suits

Kharadi has grown fast around its office parks, and many large banks and technology firms have offices here. Living close to work saves a lot of time on Nagar Road, so it suits professionals working in the area, as well as families who prefer newer societies with clubhouses and open spaces.

## Getting around

- **Roads:** Nagar Road connects Kharadi to Viman Nagar, the airport and the city. The Kharadi bypass links it towards Mundhwa and Hadapsar.
- **Airport:** roughly a 15-minute drive outside peak hours.
- **Metro:** there is no metro station in Kharadi yet. The nearest open station is Ramwadi on Line 2. An extension from Ramwadi towards Wagholi, passing Kharadi, was approved in June 2025 but is still at an early stage.

## Daily life

Kharadi has a growing choice of restaurants, cafés and supermarkets, with the larger malls of Viman Nagar a short drive away. Traffic on Nagar Road is the main complaint, and road and metro work has added to delays.

## Before you rent or buy

1. **Time your commute at peak hours,** especially if you cross Nagar Road.
2. **Ask about construction nearby.** Some pockets still have ongoing projects and road work.
3. **Check the society's water and power backup.**
4. **For a new flat, check the project on MahaRERA.** See [how to check MahaRERA registration](/blog/check-maharera-registration).

## Nearby alternatives

[Viman Nagar](/locality/viman-nagar) is more established and closer to the airport. [Magarpatta](/locality/magarpatta) and [Hadapsar](/locality/hadapsar) lie to the south. Wadgaon Sheri and Wagholi are nearby options that often offer more space.
