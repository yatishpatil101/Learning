## At a glance

- **Civic body:** Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC).
- **Known for:** leafy lanes, old bungalows, and one of Pune's best-known dining and nightlife scenes along North and South Main Road.
- **Homes:** independent bungalows, low-rise buildings and premium apartments.
- **Best for:** professionals, expats and families looking for a premium, central address.

## Who it suits

Koregaon Park is one of Pune's most upmarket neighbourhoods. Its tree-lined streets, central location and restaurants draw senior professionals, expats and families who want to be close to the city, the railway station and the airport.

## Getting around

- **Railway station:** roughly 4 km away.
- **Airport:** roughly 6 km away.
- **Metro:** the nearest open stations are on Line 2, including Bund Garden, Ruby Hall Clinic and Kalyani Nagar.
- **Roads:** bridges connect it to Kalyani Nagar and Yerawada, and Nagar Road leads east towards Viman Nagar and Kharadi.

## Daily life

The area is known for its cafés, restaurants and green, quiet residential lanes away from the main roads. Weekend evenings on the main roads can be busy and noisy, so the exact lane matters.

## Before you rent or buy

1. **Visit on a weekend evening** to judge noise and parking.
2. **For bungalows and older buildings, check the condition** and who pays for repairs.
3. **Agree everything in writing,** including the deposit, maintenance and lock-in. See our [security deposit guide](/blog/security-deposit-pune).
4. **Ask about the society's rules** on pets, guests and renovations.

## Nearby alternatives

[Viman Nagar](/locality/viman-nagar) is lively and close to the airport, and [Magarpatta](/locality/magarpatta) is a planned township to the south-east. Kalyani Nagar and Boat Club Road are close by with a similar feel.
