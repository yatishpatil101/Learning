## At a glance

- **Civic body:** Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC).
- **Known for:** one of Pune's established, upmarket residential areas, with restaurants, gyms and Westend Mall.
- **Homes:** a mix of older bungalows, established societies and newer apartments.
- **Best for:** families and professionals who want a settled, well-served neighbourhood close to the University area.

## Who it suits

Aundh offers a calmer, more established feel than its newer neighbours, while still being close to Baner and the routes to Hinjawadi. It suits families who want good everyday amenities and professionals who work in Aundh, Baner, Shivajinagar or the University area.

## Getting around

- **Roads:** Aundh is well connected to Baner, Pimple Saudagar, Shivajinagar and the University. The Aundh–Ravet BRT road links it towards Wakad and the PCMC side.
- **Highway:** the Mumbai–Bengaluru highway and the Mumbai–Pune Expressway are within reach.
- **Metro:** the nearest stations will be on Pune Metro Line 3 near the University area. The line was not yet open as of early October 2026.

## Daily life

Westend Mall near Parihar Chowk has shopping, a multiplex and dining, and the streets around it have plenty of cafés, restaurants and gyms. Schools and hospitals are within easy reach.

## Before you rent or buy

1. **Check the building's age** and the society's maintenance record.
2. **Ask about parking,** especially in older buildings.
3. **Test the commute** if you will travel to Hinjawadi or the east of the city.
4. **Agree the deposit and lock-in in writing.** See our [rent agreement guide](/blog/rent-agreement-pune).

## Nearby alternatives

[Baner](/locality/baner) and [Balewadi](/locality/balewadi) have newer societies. [Kothrud](/locality/kothrud) is another settled family area, and [Pimple Saudagar](/locality/pimple-saudagar) is across the river. Pashan is a quieter option nearby.
