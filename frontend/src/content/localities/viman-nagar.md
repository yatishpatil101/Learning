## At a glance

- **Civic body:** Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC).
- **Known for:** being right next to Pune Airport, with Phoenix Marketcity and one of the city's busiest dining and shopping stretches.
- **Homes:** a mix of established societies and newer buildings.
- **Best for:** frequent flyers, professionals and families who want convenience and a lively neighbourhood.

## Who it suits

Viman Nagar is one of east Pune's most established residential areas. It suits people who travel often, those who work in Kalyani Nagar, Yerawada, Kharadi or the city, and anyone who values restaurants, cafés and shopping close to home. Colleges such as Symbiosis Law School bring a student crowd too.

## Getting around

- **Airport:** right next door, which is the area's biggest draw.
- **Railway station:** roughly 7 to 8 km away.
- **Roads:** Nagar Road connects it to Kharadi and the city, but it is one of Pune's busiest roads.
- **Metro:** there is no station in Viman Nagar itself. The nearest open stations are Ramwadi and Kalyani Nagar on Line 2. An extension from Ramwadi towards Wagholi was approved in June 2025.

## Daily life

Everything is close by: Phoenix Marketcity, supermarkets, schools, hospitals and a long list of restaurants. The trade-off is traffic on Nagar Road, and aircraft noise in some pockets near the runway.

## Before you rent or buy

1. **Visit at different times of day** to judge traffic and aircraft noise.
2. **Check the building's age and condition,** especially in older societies.
3. **Ask about parking.** Older buildings may have limited space.
4. **Agree the deposit terms in writing.** Read our [security deposit guide](/blog/security-deposit-pune).

## Nearby alternatives

[Kharadi](/locality/kharadi) has newer societies near the IT parks. [Koregaon Park](/locality/koregaon-park) is leafier and more premium. Kalyani Nagar and Wadgaon Sheri are close by.
