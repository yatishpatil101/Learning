## At a glance

- **Civic body:** Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC). Magarpatta is a township within Hadapsar.
- **Known for:** a large, self-contained gated township with its own offices (Magarpatta CyberCity), a hospital, schools, the Aditi Gardens park and Seasons Mall nearby.
- **Management:** the township is managed by its developer company, alongside the individual housing societies.
- **Best for:** families and IT professionals who want a planned, walkable neighbourhood.

## Who it suits

Magarpatta is one of Pune's best-known planned townships. Many residents walk to work at CyberCity, children can walk to school, and the large central park and internal roads make it feel calmer than the busy roads outside. It suits families with children and professionals who work in the township or nearby in Hadapsar and Kharadi.

## Getting around

- **Inside:** most daily needs are within walking or cycling distance.
- **Roads:** Solapur Road connects it to the city, and the Kharadi bypass links it to Kharadi and Nagar Road.
- **Metro:** there is no metro here yet. Line 4, through Hadapsar, was approved in November 2025 but has not been built.

## Daily life

The township has shops, restaurants, sports facilities and green spaces of its own, and Seasons Mall and Amanora Mall are close by. Inside the township, life is quieter, but traffic at the gates and on Solapur Road can be heavy at peak hours.

## Before you rent or buy

1. **Ask about township charges** as well as society maintenance.
2. **Check the township's rules** on visitors, parking and renovations.
3. **Compare buildings within the township.** Age, floor and view make a big difference.
4. **For a resale flat, check the title papers.** See [Index II explained](/blog/index-2-pune-property).

## Nearby alternatives

[Hadapsar](/locality/hadapsar) surrounds it and offers more budget choice. [Kharadi](/locality/kharadi) is across the river. Amanora is another township next door, and [Koregaon Park](/locality/koregaon-park) is closer to the city.
